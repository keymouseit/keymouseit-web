# Claude Design Prompt — DearDev Hero Section Redesign

Redesign the hero section of my software development agency website. The single goal of this section is **conversion**: turn a first-time visitor into someone who books a discovery call. Optimize every decision for that.

## About the business
- **DearDev** (Dubai entity of a 10-year agency, KeyMouse IT, Chandigarh). Full-stack software agency, now AI-first.
- We ship web apps, mobile apps, and custom software for SaaS founders, CTOs, and operations leads — not for developers.
- Real proof points (use these, not vanity metrics): 60+ products shipped over 10 years; live App Store / Play Store apps in 15+ countries; deep experience in regulated/sensitive domains (healthcare, government); brand work including Mercedes-Benz and a Caterpillar dealer (William Adams); international clients across the US, UAE, and South/Southeast Asia.

## Conversion strategy (follow this, do not revert to generic agency layout)
1. **Headline = client outcome, not our activity.** Do NOT say "We build web apps, mobile apps, and scalable software systems." Replace with a positioning line about what the client *gets* (e.g. shipping reliable products fast, building in regulated domains without breaking, etc.). Give me 3 headline options of different angles.
2. **Proof carries the conversion.** Directly under the headline, surface a trust strip using NAMED credibility: "Live in 15+ countries · Healthcare & government-grade · Brands like Mercedes-Benz." A logo wall or named-domain row beats abstract stats. If you keep a stat row, use it as secondary support, not the main proof.
3. **One dominant CTA.** "Book a discovery call" is the primary button, high contrast, unmissable. "See selected work" becomes a quiet secondary text link, not an equal-weight button.
4. **Specificity builds trust.** A CTO scanning for 5 seconds should learn what makes us different (regulated-domain experience, shipped-and-live apps), not read adjectives.

## Aesthetic direction (reference these agencies)
- **Clay (clay.global):** premium dark canvas, huge confident type, generous negative space, restraint, immediate recognizable-client proof.
- **Halo Lab (halo-lab.com):** outcome-led headline, a single clear CTA, a showreel/visual focal point, "trusted by" logo strip right in the fold.
- **Ramotion (ramotion.com):** clean structured hierarchy, professional, blue-forward, case-study driven.
- Common DNA to capture: confident typography, lots of breathing room, premium/expensive feel, proof in the fold, zero clutter. Avoid generic AI-template aesthetics.

## Visual system
- **Theme:** dark.
- **Colors:**
  - Canvas `#0A0D14` (near-black, faint blue cast — never pure black)
  - Card / elevated surface `#12161F`
  - Hairlines / borders: white at ~8% opacity
  - Primary text `#F4F5F7`; muted text `#8A92A6`
  - Primary accent / CTA `#43BDFA` → `#477DF0`
  - ONE focal gradient only (a single element, e.g. the CTA or a highlight word): `#477DF0 → #8E41C9`
  - No standalone green — recolor any checkmarks/accents to the blue accent so the brand reads as one color story.
- **Typography:** Inter (Regular / Bold / Extra Bold). Oversized headline, tight leading, confident. Monospace label accents are acceptable for small eyebrow/label text only.
- Keep it responsive: design the desktop layout but specify how the headline, proof strip, and CTA stack on mobile.

## Layout
- Left column: eyebrow label, oversized outcome headline, one-line supporting subhead, primary CTA + secondary text link.
- Right column or below: a single premium focal element — either a "what clients hire us for" card (kept, but restyled dark and tightened) or a logo/trust strip. Do not crowd the fold.
- Whatever you choose, the headline, the named proof, and the primary CTA must all be visible without scrolling.

## Deliverable
Give me the redesigned hero on the canvas, with the 3 headline options, and briefly note the rationale for the layout choices so I can iterate.
